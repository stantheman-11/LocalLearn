Math

Competitions, local programs, and books for teens who want to go deeper than the classroom.

Where to Start

  • Khan Academy Free, self-paced courses covering everything from arithmetic to multivariable calculus. A reliable foundation for any level. khanacademy.org →
  • Brilliant.org Interactive courses built around problem-solving rather than passive reading. Especially good for building mathematical intuition. brilliant.org →

Local Opportunities

  • UCLA Math Circle A math circle run out of UCLA where students tackle challenging, competition-style problems together. A great way to go beyond standard curriculum in a community setting.
  • SMC Dual Enrollment Take actual college-level math courses at Santa Monica College while still in high school — one of the most cost-effective ways to get ahead. View course catalog →
  • SMC Summer Math Boot Camp An intensive summer program through Santa Monica College to strengthen math skills and get a head start on the coming year. smc.edu/summer-programs →
  • Mathnasium — Santa Monica A structured math learning center with a personalized approach. Good for building a solid foundation or working on specific gaps. mathnasium.com →

Competitions

  • AMC — American Mathematics Competition The MAA's flagship competition series, with separate exams for 8th grade (AMC 8), 10th grade (AMC 10), and 12th grade (AMC 12). A national benchmark and a great motivator for deeper study. maa.org/amc →
  • Purple Comet Math Meet A free, team-based online competition open to middle and high schoolers. Problems are challenging and collaborative — a fun way to compete without the pressure of individual testing. purplecomet.org →

Books

  • Art of Problem Solving Series A comprehensive curriculum spanning algebra, geometry, precalculus, calculus, and more — written specifically for students who want to go beyond the standard textbook. The gold standard for competition math preparation. artofproblemsolving.com →
  • Calculus — Michael Spivak Widely considered one of the finest calculus books ever written. Rigorous and demanding — best approached after a solid precalculus foundation. Treats calculus as mathematics, not just computation. View on Amazon →
  • How to Solve It — George Pólya A short, legendary book on mathematical problem-solving strategy. Less about specific topics and more about how to think — useful at any level.
  • The Art & Craft of Problem Solving — Paul Zeitz A deeper guide to mathematical problem-solving, bridging the gap between competition math and serious mathematical thinking.
  • Geometry Revisited — Coxeter & Greitzer A beautiful treatment of classical geometry that reveals how much depth sits beneath familiar high school material. A favorite among competition mathematicians.
