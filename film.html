Hub · Los Angeles

Film

Resources, local opportunities, and hidden gems for teen film lovers in LA.

Where to Start

  • Letterboxd An online community of film lovers where you can log your favorite films and discover ones you've never seen before. letterboxd.com →
  • Film Art: An Introduction This book will give you an introduction on how to go from watching film to analyzing it. Check your local library.
  • Sight & Sound's Greatest Films of All Time The renowned magazine's aggregate list of the greatest achievements in filmmaking, from the 1920s to today. View list →

Expand Your Knowledge

  • The Cinema Book A guide to the next steps in furthering your knowledge of film as an artistic discipline. Available at many libraries.
  • Oxford History of World Cinema A comprehensive historical survey of film to contextualize your watching within the broader canon.
  • What Is Cinema? — André Bazin A foundational text of film theory, exploring how movies affect viewers and society as a whole.

Local Opportunities

  • Teens of LA Film Festival Showcase your films at this annual festival for teens, run by the LA Public Library. Completely free to submit. lapl.org →
  • USC School of Cinematic Arts Summer Program A comprehensive filmmaking course using the resources of USC's renowned film program. Paid. cinema.usc.edu →
  • TeenTix LA Discounted arts and entertainment tickets for teens across the LA area. teentix.org →

Hidden Gems

  • Kanopy A completely free streaming service accessible with your library card. Huge library of classic and contemporary films — almost nobody knows about it. kanopy.com →
  • Academy Museum LA Frequently screens classic films in a state-of-the-art theater. Discounted $5 tickets for students. academymuseum.org →

Publications & Extra Resources

  • JSTOR A wide selection of academic texts, including many on film. Not public — but many schools have institutional access you might not know about. jstor.org →
  • Sight & Sound One of the most historic film publications. Some articles are free online; full archive access via subscription. bfi.org.uk/sight-and-sound →
  • The Criterion Collection Publishes free scholarly essays online for many of their releases — a great way to go deeper on a film. criterion.com/current →
  • Senses of Cinema sensesofcinema.com →
  • Film Comment filmcomment.com →
  • Jonathan Rosenbaum Renowned film critic with a large archive of writing freely available on his website. jonathanrosenbaum.net →
